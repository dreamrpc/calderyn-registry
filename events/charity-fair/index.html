Before You Write — OOC Notice

Please read

A few ground rules before scenes begin. These apply to everyone for the duration of the fair.

  • The cameras are rolling. This is a public, press-covered event — characters are on the record, so play it as a watched, on-camera moment.
  • This is a children's charity fair. Handle every scene with care and keep all interactions with the kids wholesome, warm, and entirely appropriate.
  • No inappropriate, suggestive, or adult content involving minors of any kind. This is a hard line and is non-negotiable.
  • Stick to the itinerary and be where you need to be. Keep your character in the right place at the right time so scenes line up and nobody's left waiting.
  • Follow all roleplay.chat rules at all times.

03 Ground Floor

Games Zone

Ground Floor
You're playing for a kid. When you reach the games zone, your character is paired with a child from the hospital wards. You invent that kid — give them a name, a personality, the superpower they have, and why they're here being treated. Then you play every stall to win prizes for them.

Run it solo, or team up with friends and play for the same kid together. Every roll is for them, and the bigger the win, the bigger the prize you bring back to their bedside.
D20
Roll a D20 — higher is better.
A natural 20 always wins the jackpot prize. Prizes go directly to your paired kid.

05 Hospital Wards

Ward Visits

Anytime
Free RP · No roll required. Characters can slip away to the wards at any point during the event — before arrival scenes wrap, between games, or after the cheer performance. Keep these scenes warm, respectful, and character-focused. The kids are what this is all about.
06 GOSH → Calderyn

Bus Home

8:30 PM
No rolls. The fair winds down around 8:30 PM and the coach reassembles outside. Let scenes close naturally — quiet goodbyes, characters reflecting on the night on the ride back. No pressure, no clock. The coach departs when writers are done.
